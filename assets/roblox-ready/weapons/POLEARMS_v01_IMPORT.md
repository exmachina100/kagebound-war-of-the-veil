# Polearm original mesh exports v01
Generated as actual editable ASCII Wavefront OBJ geometry plus MTL palette, not concept art or Roblox-ready verified imports.

- Naginata: original long shaft, curved-profile simplified blade, gold collar, red grip. Y-up, center grip at x/z=0, extends from y=-3.3 to 5.6.
- Yari: original spear shaft, elongated spear head, gold ferrule, red grip. Y-up, center grip at x/z=0, extends from y=-3.3 to 5.9.
- Units are modeling units; scale and Grip attachment must be calibrated after Studio import. Static meshes, no rigs, no animations, no textures/UVs.
- Both OBJ and MTL must be kept together. Convert to GLB/FBX in Blender if needed. Roblox Studio import, collision, handedness and gameplay fit NOT verified.
- Distinct gameplay archetypes are currently playtest prototypes. Asset parity and Studio QA remain outstanding.
