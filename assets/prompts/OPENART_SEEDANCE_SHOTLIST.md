# OpenArt / Seedance visual references

Render all shots in stylized **Roblox block-character proportions**, consistent costumes, camera and attack VFX. These are **prompts**, not completed videos.

## Shot 01 — Katana duel
16:9, 8 seconds. Third-person gameplay camera behind black/red Shinobi with white Kitsune mask, facing slate-blue Ronin under a torii in Moonfall Bamboo. Three readable katana strikes with narrow red crescent trails, Ronin performs an electric-blue perfect parry, distinct impact sparks. 60 FPS Roblox gameplay aesthetic, no cinematic cuts, no realistic human proportions, no UI text artifacts.

## Shot 02 — Kitsune tactical illusion
16:9, 8 seconds. Same arena and characters, fixed third-person camera. Shinobi dashes left with red scarf flutter, one pale-pink fox spirit duplicate continues forward. Ronin swings at illusion while real player circles to flank. Show ability cooldown and clean silhouettes. Roblox-style movement, crisp VFX, no camera cut.

## Shot 03 — Veilfall
16:9, 8 seconds. Gameplay camera tracks duel in Moonfall Bamboo. Violet tear appears in moonlit sky, torii gate lights pulse, new glowing floating platforms materialize at upper edges of arena, players dash and jump into new path. Clear beginning/middle/end and readable traversable geometry. No teleport cuts.

## Negative constraints
No realistic anatomy, no hyper-detailed impossible geometry, no giant obscuring explosions, no character or costume changes between shots, no fake buttons or uncontrollable camera, no logos from third-party anime titles.
