## 2. Combat system: fast but deeply skill-based

Players fight in third-person, with responsive controls and short, intense rounds.

The fundamentals should be accessible to new Roblox players while leaving room for competitive mastery.

| Mechanic       | Gameplay                                |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Light attack   | Quick three-hit combinations            |
| Heavy attack   | Slow, powerful guard-breaking strike    |
| Perfect parry  | Timed defense that creates an opening   |
| Dash           | Short directional evasive movement      |
| Wall run       | Ninja movement across selected surfaces |
| Double jump    | Aerial positioning and attack setup     |
| Grapple        | Reach roofs, platforms, and enemies     |
| Spirit ability | Special attack determined by mask       |
| Veil Burst     | Charged ultimate earned through combat  |

### Combat philosophy

We would avoid excessive stun-locking and one-button kills. A beginner should be able to win a fight through good positioning, while expert players can combine wall movement, timed parries, aerial attacks, and mask abilities.

## 11. A more ambitious direction

Once the foundation is strong, I would push KAGEBOUND beyond the conventional arena fighter.

Imagine a living Japanese mythological world where the dojo is also a social hub. Players challenge each other to rooftop duels, discover hidden Yokai, unlock combat scrolls from legendary masters, and participate in world events.

Some particularly interesting expansions:

- Possession combat: A spirit temporarily alters your fighting style with an entirely different animation set.
- Sword clashes: When two perfectly timed heavy attacks meet, a brief clash opens an opportunity for either fighter to gain advantage through timing.
- Spirit fusion: Teammates synchronize abilities to unlock spectacular combination attacks.
- Living bosses: Giant mythological Yokai invade the lobby during special events.
- Rivalries: Players can build competitive histories with recurring opponents and earn cosmetic rivalry titles.

The central identity would remain Japanese swordsmanship, supernatural trickery, and high-skill competitive combat.

## 12. The development roadmap

1. Combat prototype: Build one Roblox arena with two characters, katana attacks, parries, dodges, and server-validated hit detection.
2. Movement prototype: Add responsive ninja traversal, wall running, aerial attacks, and grapple mechanics.
3. Mythic prototype: Introduce Kitsune, Oni, and Tengu abilities, followed by the first Veilfall transformation.
4. Playable multiplayer: Add two modes, round management, loadouts, the dojo, and basic player progression.
5. Content production: Expand to eight weapons, four styles, two polished arenas, animations, VFX, and audio.
6. Alpha and balancing: Test with real players, tune combat responsiveness and fairness, validate performance on mobile, desktop, and console.
7. Release preparation: Add onboarding, progression balancing, cosmetic monetization, safety controls, analytics, and launch content.

The most important decision: We should build the combat prototype before creating dozens of warriors, maps, or legendary weapons. If sword fighting, movement, and parrying feel exceptional, the mythology can turn that foundation into a distinctive game.
