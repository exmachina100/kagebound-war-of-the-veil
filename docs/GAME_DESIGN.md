# KAGEBOUND — Canonical Game Design Brief

**Working title:** KAGEBOUND: WAR OF THE VEIL  
**Tagline:** Two worlds. One battlefield. Become the legend.

## Premise
Centuries ago a supernatural veil separated the human world and Yokai realm. Warriors known as **Veilbound** fight as the boundary breaks, mixing martial skill and mythological powers.

## Core differentiation
Not a Japanese-themed RIVALS firearm reskin. Fast, readable third-person melee with responsive mobility and **Veilfall**, the transformation of arenas during live rounds.

## Combat and traversal
Light three-hit combo; heavy guard breaker; frame-timed perfect parry; dash; double jump; later wall-run, grapple, aerial attacks. Reward positioning and skill; avoid stunlock loops and one-button kills.

## Warrior styles
| Style | Identity | Combat role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shinobi | speed / stealth | evasive ambush |
| Ronin | precision / parry | counters and blade mastery |
| Onmyoji | magic / traps | tactical control |
| Oni Disciple | power / resistance | slower heavy pressure |

Players choose a main weapon, optional tool, and mythic mask. Style does not permanently lock weapon choice.

## Masks
Kitsune = illusion/clone, Oni = rage/knockback, Tengu = wind/aerial. Future Yuki-onna = ice, Nekomata = deception, Kappa = water/defense. Each mask has a passive, an active and an ultimate. Gameplay powers must be earnable through play.

## Weapons
Katana, Nodachi, Tanto, Naginata, Yari, Kanabo, Kusarigama, Yumi. Additional tools: kunai, shuriken, ofuda, war fan.

## Veilfall
A predictable mid-match transition changes navigation: floating spectral bridges/platforms, altered routes, potential shifting visibility and hazards. Telegraph before collision changes; preserve fairness and competitive readability.

## Modes
Shadow Duel (1v1 best-of-five); Clan Clash (2v2 through 5v5); future Yokai Hunt cooperative bosses and Spirit Run parkour. Seasonal clan conflict and PvPvE expansion later.

## Worlds
Moonfall Bamboo (compact stone and bamboo arena), Shogun's Last City (vertical rooftops), Frozen Shrine (ice), Yomi Rift (spectral realm), plus social **Kage Dojo** hub.

## Progression
Warrior ranks, cosmetic weapon mastery, masks and variants, dojo challenges, clan identity. Avoid grind-only power escalation and pay-to-win monetization. Trading and inventory must be secure.

## Original MVP 0.1 envelope
4 styles, 8 weapons, 3 masks, 2 arenas, 2 PvP modes, 1 dojo lobby. MVP completion requires join → loadout → duel → Veilfall → result → dojo.

## Build order
Combat feel first, movement second, masks and Veilfall third, complete multiplayer match and loadouts fourth, content production fifth, balancing and release sixth.

## Visual art direction
Premium Roblox-inspired blocky character geometry, highly readable silhouettes, moonlit Japanese shrines, torii, bamboo and lanterns. Crimson = offense, blue = defensive precision, violet = supernatural/Veilfall, warm gold = world/accent. Keep VFX restrained enough for mobile PvP clarity.
